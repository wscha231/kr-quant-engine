## Separation from financial quality

The existing Korean `quality_score` is a quantitative financial factor built from
ROE, operating margin, and leverage. It is historical/PIT-tested code and is not
redefined by this work.

`moat_quality_v2` records a separate research object: source-backed evidence
about durable competitive advantage. The two must remain separate until a
preregistered incremental-alpha experiment demonstrates value out of sample.

## Six required dimensions

1. `qualification_switching_cost`
2. `market_structure_position`
3. `ip_patent_durability`
4. `pricing_power`
5. `replacement_difficulty`
6. `next_generation_relevance`

Each dimension requires a 0..1 assessment, separate confidence, primary-source
evidence with publication and availability timestamps, raw SHA256, a
counter-argument, and invalidation conditions.

Evidence must satisfy `available_at <= as_of <= reviewed_at <= cutoff`.
Missing dimensions fail closed; no neutral imputation or partial average is
allowed.

## Authority

A complete packet emits an unweighted six-dimension research index only.
It always declares:

- `historical_pit_certified=false`
- `oos_validated=false`
- `selector_eligible=false`
- `portfolio_weight_effect=0`
- target/order/production authority false

No live Korean picks, theme leader selection, weights, or order paths are changed.

## Korea-specific evidence examples

Use DART filings, company/customer disclosures, KIPO/WIPO/USPTO patent records,
court/regulatory decisions, technical standards, and source-backed industry data.
Do not infer monopoly from high margin, recent price performance, or management
claims alone.

For equipment/materials, explicitly investigate qualification time, yield/process
know-how, recipe integration, patent design-around risk, and customer dual-sourcing.
For power/shipbuilding/nuclear, include certification, installed base, capacity
scarcity, localization and replacement lead time. For cosmetics/biotech, separate
brand/platform/IP durability from temporary demand growth or pipeline optionality.
