## Separation from financial quality

The existing Korean `quality_score` is a quantitative financial factor based on
ROE, operating margin and leverage. Rev3 does not change that feature or its
historical meaning.

`moat_quality_v2` records a separate current-research object: source-backed,
time-bound evidence about durable competitive advantage. It has zero selector or
portfolio authority until a separately preregistered PIT/OOS experiment passes.

## Rev3 evidence gates

Every evidence row:
- is bound to the reviewed `asset_id` and `issuer_id`;
- carries source type, source affiliation and independent-group identity;
- preserves `published_at`, `available_at`, `verified_at`, and `valid_through`;
- is admitted only when `published_at <= available_at <= verified_at <= as_of <= reviewed_at <= trusted cutoff`;
- must remain valid at `as_of` and satisfy the source-type re-verification-age ceiling;
- supplies immutable raw bytes through a trusted resolver, whose SHA-256 is recomputed and compared with the declared digest.

Positive assessments require SUPPORT evidence from a non-issuer source. Negative
assessments require CHALLENGE evidence. A neutral 0.5 requires both. Every
dimension also requires a counter-argument, reviewed reconciliation and explicit
invalidation conditions. The full packet must span at least two independent
source groups.

This closes the six material defects found in the first PR #3 Codex review:
self-authorized cutoff, unbound issuer evidence, stale evidence, management-only
corroboration, unverified raw hashes and assessment/evidence-direction mismatch.

## Korea-specific interpretation

- Semiconductor equipment/materials: qualification duration, yield/process
  know-how, recipe integration, patent design-around, dual sourcing, next-node fit.
- Power: certifications, installed base, local manufacturing, transformer lead
  times, grid interconnection, replacement capacity.
- Shipbuilding/nuclear: engineering certification, shipyard or component slots,
  installed fleet, regulatory qualification, localization and replacement lead time.
- Cosmetics: ODM formulation database, manufacturing qualification, distribution
  switching cost, brand versus platform durability.
- Biotech: composition-of-matter/platform/process patents, freedom to operate,
  partner validation, royalty economics and clinical/regulatory invalidation.

High margin or recent share-price performance alone is never monopoly evidence.
