## Equal-comparison architecture

Every serious candidate requires the same ten pillars:

1. industry structure / bottleneck
2. moat durability
3. growth runway / customer / product
4. profitability / reinvestment / capital efficiency
5. valuation / margin of safety
6. earnings revision / operating acceleration
7. market leadership / price-volume / RS
8. management / governance / capital allocation
9. catalyst / ownership / information edge
10. downside / balance-sheet / cycle / regime

Absolute assessments use the shared `canonical-pillar-anchor-v1` scale.
Peer-relative assessment is not manually entered; it is derived from reviewed
peer rank as `(N-rank)/(N-1)`. Global industry peers are preferred. Any local
or lifecycle peer set requires an explicit exception reason.

Peer snapshots and every upstream reviewed artifact must resolve from trusted
storage and match their declared SHA-256 bytes. Current evidence must be
available no later than packet `as_of`.

## Method lenses

Classic lenses: Graham, Buffett/Munger, Fisher, Lynch/GARP, Greenblatt,
O'Neil/CANSLIM, Minervini, Porter, Damodaran, Druckenmiller, Howard Marks,
Piotroski, Soros/reflexivity and Mauboussin competitive-advantage-period.

Academic lenses: Fama-French value/profitability/investment, Jegadeesh-Titman
momentum, Novy-Marx profitability, Asness quality/value/momentum and PEAD.

Project lenses: quality compounder, market leader, early growth inflection,
turnaround value, smart-money/event and regime resilience.

All lenses reuse canonical pillars and are explanatory only. They are not added
to the equal-pillar score and cannot create selector alpha.

## Korea-specific interpretation

- semiconductors/materials/equipment: qualification, yield/process know-how,
  patent design-around, dual sourcing, roadmap/node relevance and customer concentration.
- power/grid: certification, installed base, local manufacturing, backlog,
  transformer/switchgear lead time and replacement capacity.
- shipbuilding/nuclear/project industrials: yard/component slots, backlog margin,
  project execution/cost overrun, working capital and engineering qualification.
- cosmetics/ODM: brand/ODM switching cost, formulation database, repeat demand,
  channel/inventory economics and global customer diversification.
- biotech: platform/composition/process IP, FTO, partner validation,
  probability-adjusted pipeline economics, royalty structure, cash runway and
  clinical/regulatory invalidation. PE/FCF is not forced when economically invalid.
- financials/commodities/software use the same common adapters as the U.S. contract.

Sector profiles change interpretation only, never pillar weight.

## Portfolio theory separation

alpha belong to A5 portfolio construction after reviewed ER/downside. They do
not add stock-selection points.
